# Cofiz User Guide

Cofiz tracks money moving between your **company**, its **collectors**, and
**viewers**. The app works in English and Amharic, offline and online.

## Roles

| Role | Sees and does |
|---|---|
| **Admin** | Everything: distributions, purchases, transfers, income, expenses, reports, users, settings, audit log, data export/import. |
| **Collector** | Own balance, own transactions and history; records purchases and returns; confirms incoming transfers. |
| **Viewer** | Read-only reports and data, plus own investment records. |

## Daily flows

- **Give money out (admin):** Collectors page → collector → Distribute.
- **Record a coffee purchase (collector):** Home → Purchase (deducted from your balance).
- **Return unspent money (collector):** Home → Return.
- **Move money between collectors:** Transfer picker → sender, receiver, amount.
  The receiver confirms once and both sides settle. Transfers never change the
  company balance.
- **Company income:** Income screen → investment (linked to a viewer) or manual sale.
- **Expenses:** Expenses screen → add by category.
- **Receipts:** any record can generate, share, or print a receipt.

## Protecting the app

- **App PIN:** Settings → PIN lock → Set PIN. The app locks after 2 minutes
  idle. Five wrong attempts signs you out.
- **Biometric unlock** (if your device supports it) appears on the lock screen.
- **Sign-in** uses WhatsApp/Telegram one-time codes or your PIN.

## Updates

Cofiz updates itself outside the Play Store. When a new version is published:

1. You get a notification and an **App update** card at the top of Settings.
2. Tap **Update** — the app downloads and opens the installer.
3. Confirm install; reopen the app. That's it.

Settings always shows your installed version; About shows it too.

## Offline

No connection? Keep working — records queue on-device and sync when you're
back online. A banner shows pending items.
